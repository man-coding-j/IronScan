# Third-party notices

IronScan uses Apple platform frameworks supplied with Xcode and iOS, including SwiftUI, AVFoundation, Core ML, Core Image, Accelerate, and SIMD.

The optional local diagnostics server depends on FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Jinja2, HTMX, Plotly, Uvicorn, pytest, Ruff, mypy, and their transitive dependencies. Dependency versions are locked in `uv.lock`; license inventory belongs in `compliance/dependency_licenses.csv` and must be reviewed before distribution.

No third-party participant images or private datasets are included in this repository.
