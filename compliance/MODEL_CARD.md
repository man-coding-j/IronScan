# Model card

The bundled segmentation and risk packages are deterministic synthetic engineering fixtures used to validate deployment plumbing. They are not participant-facing models and must not be interpreted as validated risk estimates. Replace them only with documented training provenance, dataset version, manifest, checksum, parity fixtures, and evaluation.

`IronScanPallorClassifier.mlpackage` is a separate MobileNetV3-Small engineering fixture trained against the TongueDX visual `TonguePale` annotation. It is loaded once for a clearly labeled live engineering preview and does not replace `AnemiaRisk.mlpackage` or produce participant-facing risk decisions. Fold-1 participant groups were separated across train/validation/test, 12 ambiguous duplicate contents were excluded, training used class weight `7.0168`, validation-only Platt calibration, and an F1-optimized validation-locked threshold of `0.162618`.

Held-out test performance was ROC-AUC `0.7872`, precision `0.2639`, recall `0.6129`, F1 `0.3689`, balanced accuracy `0.7068`, and confusion matrix `[[639, 159], [36, 57]]`. These results are insufficient for participant-facing use. The model predicts a source annotation only; it does not measure hemoglobin, ferritin, anemia, iron deficiency, or iron status. Training occurred from an uncommitted worktree, and independent standardized-capture, laboratory-paired, cohort, device, calibration, fairness, and physical-performance validation remain pending.

`IronScanMultiScreen.mlpackage` is a MobileNetV3-Small engineering fixture for the source dataset's `TonguePale`, `Toothmark`, `Crack`, `TipSideRed`, and `FurThick` visual annotations. It uses participant-separated fold-1 splits, per-output class weighting, per-output Platt calibration, and per-output locked thresholds. Held-out ROC-AUC values range from `0.7131` to `0.8639`; `TonguePale` precision remains `0.2591`. The application loads it once for a clearly labeled live engineering preview and reports only locked-threshold visual-annotation comparisons. It must not drive `RiskPrediction` or be interpreted as evidence of diseases, nutrient deficiencies, hydration status, inflammation, oral-health conditions, anemia, or iron status.

`IronScanEye.mlpackage`, `IronScanNail.mlpackage`, and `IronScanTongue.mlpackage` are separate MobileNetV3-Small engineering fixtures for the three-step competition demo. They use source-repository proxy classes, validation-only Platt calibration, locked per-model thresholds, and Core ML parity differences below `0.000005`. Eye and nail version `0.2.0-engineering.1` were retrained after deterministic appearance clustering; their held-out proxy ROC-AUC, precision, recall, and F1 were all `1.0000`. Tongue remains version `0.1.0-engineering.1`, with ROC-AUC `0.8167`, precision `0.2908`, recall `0.6628`, and F1 `0.4043`.

The perfect eye and nail proxy metrics do not demonstrate scientific validity. These small sources may contain augmented or related images, proxy classes may align with repository-specific capture artifacts, and appearance clustering cannot recover missing participant identities. The evaluation is group-disjoint by deterministic similarity cluster, but it is not verified participant-independent or externally validated. None of these models estimates anemia, iron deficiency, hemoglobin, ferritin, or iron status.

`IronScanEye_Clinical.mlpackage` version `0.3.1-engineering.1` is a separate clinical-label engineering fixture trained on 216 local `Eyes-Defy-Anemia` palpebral images. Its binary label is derived from source laboratory hemoglobin using adult sex-specific anemia-screening thresholds. The held-out group-disjoint test had ROC-AUC `0.7798`, precision `0.5357`, recall `0.8333`, F1 `0.6522`, balanced accuracy `0.6759`, and confusion matrix `[[14, 13], [3, 15]]`. These results are exploratory, from a small two-country cohort, and do not establish iron-deficiency anemia or participant-facing performance.

The uncertainty-enabled exports add a `predictive_uncertainty` output. Training uses dropout probability `0.2`; export evaluates 30 fixed Bernoulli masks at the classifier head and returns the calibrated probability mean and standard deviation in one Core ML call. Fixed masks make package inference reproducible and exportable, but repeated package calls are not fresh stochastic draws. Validation locks the uncertainty threshold at the 75th percentile. Eye Core ML parity differences were at most `0.0000152` for logits, `0.0000064` for probability, and `0.0000005` for uncertainty. The updated nail proxy fixture had test ROC-AUC `1.0000`, precision `1.0000`, recall `0.9333`, and F1 `0.9655`. The updated tongue visual-pallor proxy fixture had test ROC-AUC `0.7951`, precision `0.2542`, recall `0.6977`, and F1 `0.3727`. These proxy metrics remain unsuitable evidence of clinical validity.

Eye version `0.3.1-engineering.1` adds Vision face-landmark ROI selection before model input. The app selects the eye nearest the frame center and extracts the lower 25 percent of its landmark bounding box as an experimental conjunctiva ROI before manifest-locked resizing. This acquisition adapter has no physical-device golden set and was not part of the original training evaluation; performance must be re-established on standardized physical captures before any validation claim.
